\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Specialist CD architectures.}
Early CD methods extended Siamese U-Nets with feature differencing
(FC-EF, FC-Siam-conc/diff~\citep{daudt2018fully}) or spatio-temporal attention
(STANet~\citep{chen2020spatial}). IFNet~\citep{zhang2020deeply} introduced
deeply-supervised fusion; SNUNet~\citep{fang2021snunet} proposed a nested
Siamese U-Net. The transformer era brought BIT~\citep{chen2021remote},
ChangeFormer~\citep{bandara2022changeformer}, and
ChangerEx~\citep{fang2023changer}. TinyCD~\citep{codegoni2023tinycd} showed
that aggressive architectural compression can match larger baselines with
0.28~M parameters. More recently, ChangeStar~\citep{zheng2022changestar} and
Changen~\citep{zheng2023changen} demonstrate the value of synthetic
pretraining, while DDPM-CD~\citep{bandara2024ddpmcd} extracts features from a
pretrained diffusion model.

\paragraph{Foundation models for change detection.}
Several works freeze or lightly adapt large self-supervised backbones.
TTP~\citep{chen2024ttp} uses LoRA on SAM's image encoder and attains 92.1 F1
on LEVIR-CD. BAN~\citep{li2024ban} freezes CLIP ViT-L and learns a small
bi-temporal adapter on top. SAM-CD~\citep{dingsamcd} combines a ViT encoder
with SAM's mask decoder for CD. AnyChange~\citep{zheng2024segment} explores
zero-shot change detection with SAM; it does not fine-tune but instead uses
point-prompt heuristics. UniChange~\citep{zeng2025unichange} extends to
multi-modal LLM-conditioned CD. SChanger~\citep{sun2025schanger} reports the
current LEVIR-CD SOTA (92.87 F1) with a custom Siamese head.
Our work differs in two key respects: (i) we systematically study
\emph{pairings} of encoder and decoder families rather than fixing one; and
(ii) we leverage the \emph{dense-prompt slot} of the SAM decoder, which prior
SAM-based CD work has not exploited.

\paragraph{Datasets and evaluation.}
LEVIR-CD~\citep{chen2020spatial} remains the most-reported benchmark
(7120/1024/2048 train/val/test 256$^2$ crops). Larger variants include
LEVIR-CD+~\citep{zheng2022changestar}, S2Looking~\citep{shen2021s2looking}
(rural, off-nadir), and CDD~\citep{lebedev2018change} (4 seasons).
DSIFN-CD~\citep{zhang2020deeply} and SECOND~\citep{yang2021second} cover
multi-city and multi-class settings respectively; we follow standard practice
and report the binary-CD collapse for SECOND. A recent reality-check
study~\citep{corley2024reality} shows that F1 scores on CDD have saturated
past the point of statistical significance for single-seed comparisons; we
report IoU alongside F1 on CDD for this reason.
