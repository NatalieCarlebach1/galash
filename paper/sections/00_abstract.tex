Bi-temporal change detection in aerial and satellite imagery has been dominated
by task-specific convolutional and transformer architectures, while the broader
vision community has converged on large self-supervised backbones such as
\textsc{DINOv2}, \textsc{DINOv3}, and the \textsc{SAM} family. We ask whether
these foundation models can be \emph{composed} --- rather than fine-tuned ---
to obtain competitive change-detection performance. We introduce
\textbf{GALASH}, a unified pipeline that pairs any frozen \textsc{DINO}-style
encoder with any \textsc{SAM}-family mask decoder via two lightweight
trainable modules: (i) a \emph{cross-change attention} operator that uses a
learnable temperature to produce per-patch change tokens from reference and
target features, and (ii) a multi-scale \emph{Bridge v2} that fuses DINO
features into SAM's prompt and image-embedding interfaces, repurposing the
normally-unused dense-prompt slot as a change-conditioning signal. Our
encoder/decoder registry spans \textbf{16+ encoders $\times$ 7+ decoders}
(four \textsc{DINO} generations including remote-sensing and satellite
pretraining, two \textsc{SAM} generations), giving a principled testbed for
studying which foundation-model pairings transfer best to change detection.
GALASH trains only $\sim$\textbf{16.5 M parameters} on top of frozen
backbones (3$\times$ fewer than ChangeFormer, 27$\times$ fewer than DDPM-CD)
and achieves \textbf{state-of-the-art} on
SECOND (\textbf{74.30} F1, $+1.18$ over UniChange's 73.12) and DSIFN-CD
(\textbf{96.74} F1, $+0.09$ over DDPM-CD's 96.65), and is competitive on
LEVIR-CD (\textbf{90.89} F1) and CDD (\textbf{96.76} F1). A surprising finding
is that SAM decoder \emph{capacity is irrelevant} for change detection:
SAM2.1-tiny (39 M) matches or beats SAM2.1-large (225 M) within $0.3$ F1.
We also find that \emph{newer DINO generations} matter more than parameter
scale: DINOv2-RS-base (86 M, RS-pretrained) outperforms DINOv2-large (304 M,
generic) by $4.7$ F1 on LEVIR-CD; DINOv3-large further closes the SOTA gap
on hard benchmarks (LEVIR-CD+ $+1.5$, S2Looking $+3.2$ over DINOv2-RS).
We release code, checkpoints, sweep logs, and per-dataset YAML configs that
match each benchmark's published SOTA augmentation pipeline verbatim ---
enforcing fair comparison.
